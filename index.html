Medical Device QA Handbook

QA HANDBOOK

A pocket handbook for QA professionals working on Class II and III medical devices, in four parts. Basics walks through ISO 13485 clause by clause with best practices. Part A covers what changed under the FDA QMSR in each QMS discipline. Part B covers FDA inspection, postmarket and premarket requirements. Part C covers the EU MDR.
