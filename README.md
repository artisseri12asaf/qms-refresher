# Medical Device QA Handbook

A pocket handbook for QA professionals working on Class II and III medical devices: ISO 13485 basics, the FDA QMSR, FDA premarket and postmarket requirements, and the EU MDR.

- 24 chapters with takeaways, 504 questions, debates, 735 real-world dilemmas and official sources
- Training mode, selected dilemmas, keyword search and a performance indicator
- QMS map: ISO 13485:2016 ↔ 21 CFR 820 (QMSR) ↔ EU MDR 2017/745, with coverage and exemptions
- Sample size assistant: ISO 2859-1 normal single sampling plans, and confidence/reliability sample sizes

Open the site, then use "Add to Home Screen" on your phone to install it as an app. It works offline after the first visit. Answers are saved in the browser.
